Bring the International Skill Championship to your school

ISC 2026 is a national championship for Classes 5–12, across four very different tracks. The school screening round is free, fully online, and open to every eligible student you have.

One teacher registers as your school’s coordinator, and your students take it from there.

Four tracks

There is a track for every kind of student

AI for Impact

Build a working app or prototype that solves a real problem. Teams of up to three.

Young Entrepreneurship

Pitch an original idea, from the problem all the way to the business model.

Puzzle Master

Timed online logic and reasoning. Individual entry, in two class divisions.

Content Creator

One original sixty-second video on this year’s theme. Teams of up to three.

Why schools take part

 
 Free to enter. The school screening round costs your school nothing.
 
 No cap on entries. Enter as many students as you like — the top three per track, per school, go through.
 
 Real recognition. National winners receive funding of up to ₹1 lakh, deployment support for their app, brand campaign features, and a ₹2 lakh Puzzle Master prize pool.
 
 English or Hindi. Both accepted equally, across all four tracks.
 
 Students keep their work. Parental consent is required for every under-18 participant.
