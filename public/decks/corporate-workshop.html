Responsible AI
Your team learns the 3 rules
before they touch any tool.
Why This Matters
90% of AI risk incidents trace back to employees not understanding what the tool can and cannot do. Training — not technology — is the fix.
Rule 1 · Data Privacy
Never paste confidential data into public AI.
No client names. No financials. No internal docs. No personal info.
Rule 2 · Authority
AI drafts. You decide.
AI is your assistant, not your boss. Every output needs your judgment.
Rule 3 · Verify
Always verify AI output.
✓ Check facts & numbers
✓ Spot hallucinations
✓ Confirm tone & accuracy
✓ Never send without reading
Built in, not bolted on. A live hallucination-spotting exercise is part of the workshop.
