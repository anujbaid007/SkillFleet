SMART Prompt
I'm a Class 10 CBSE student. Give me 5 essay

07 / 09
Responsible AI
Every parent's real worry about AI
— answered in 3 rules.
Why This Matters
88% of students use AI for assessments — up from 53% last year. Without training, AI becomes a shortcut that kills critical thinking. Training — not banning — is the answer.
Rule 1 · Integrity
AI helps you learn. Never cheats for you.
Don't copy AI answers. Use AI to understand, brainstorm, and improve your own work.
Rule 2 · Privacy
Never share personal data with AI.
No real names, phone numbers, addresses, or school details in any AI tool.
Rule 3 · Verify
AI can be wrong. Always fact-check.
✓ Cross-check with textbook
✓ Spot hallucinated facts
✓ Verify dates & numbers
✓ Never submit without reading
Built in, not bolted on. A live “spot the AI mistake” challenge is part of the workshop.
