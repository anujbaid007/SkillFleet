Responsible AI
Every principal's real worry about AI
— answered in 3 rules.
Grounded in UNESCO Guidance
Source
" Generative AI in education needs human capacity building, regulation, teacher empowerment, and responsible use.
Rule 1 · Privacy
No student data in public AI tools.
No names. No marks. No phone numbers. No personal details.
Rule 2 · Authority
AI drafts. Teacher decides.
AI is the assistant. The teacher is always the author.
Rule 3 · Verification
Every output must pass 4 checks.
✓ Factual accuracy
✓ Bias check
✓ Language level
✓ Board relevance
Built in, not bolted on. A live hallucination-correction drill is part of the workshop.
